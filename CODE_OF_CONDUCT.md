# Community conduct

Treat contributors and players with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people. Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

Welcome newcomers, respect different experience levels and keep feedback specific and constructive. This applies to repository issues, reviews and other spaces maintained for the project.

Report concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile contact options. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, limit interaction or block repeated or serious misconduct. Apply these rules consistently, preserve reporter privacy where possible, and do not retaliate against reports made in good faith.
