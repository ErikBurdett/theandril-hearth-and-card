## Trigger window · October 30, 2026

The last open duel rule. Automatic triggers (Gather, Fellowship lifegain, Spellcraft, Welcome, Recordwork, Mourning) now wait on the stack with a response window instead of applying the instant their event happens. One event wakes one item per owner naming every source, so a window costs one pass; the active player's items wait first so the other side's resolve first; triggers cannot be countered; and when the window closes a trigger applies once per source still on the table and fades for the rest, so felling a Gather companion in response stops its growth and felling a mourner stops its drain. Spellcraft waits above the spell that woke it; a mutual Mourning drain is no longer a simultaneous draw but resolves one side at a time. Rivals yield after a resource that wakes a trigger, shield a lethal Mourning drain when they can and otherwise let triggers resolve; the keeper's autoplay passes them; in assisted play the rival answers a keeper's trigger at once, as it answers a spell. Stack items gain optional `trigger` and `sources` fields, so every older save and the published-build fixtures load unchanged.
