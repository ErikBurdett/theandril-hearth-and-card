## 01 · Next development priorities

Build on the working duel and tavern. The order below is the proposed sequence; there are no promised dates.

- [x] **Completed — Finish the deeper duel rules** (battle-rules)
  - Current: Saved assisted/Full control priority, consecutive passes, payment previews, cleanup choices and drawn results are implemented. Each attacker now chooses its own destination, any number of blockers may share an attacker, the attacking player orders damage among them, Overrun spills past every blocker's lethal share, and rivals gang-block and order damage from the public table. Older saves with single blockers load as one-entry lists. Automatic triggers (Gather, Fellowship lifegain, Spellcraft, Welcome, Recordwork, Mourning) now wait on the stack with a response window: the triggers one event wakes share one item, the active player's wait first so the other side's resolve first, they cannot be countered, and a trigger fades for every source that left the table before it resolved. Rivals shield a lethal Mourning drain and otherwise let triggers resolve; the keeper's autoplay passes them.
  - Completion boundary: Delivered: per-attacker destinations, ordered multiple blockers, queued trigger timing with a response window, save/reload continuity and the same rules on both seats, verified in simulation and Chromium.
  - Evidence: [src/sim/battle.ts](https://github.com/ErikBurdett/theandril-hearth-and-card/blob/main/src/sim/battle.ts), [src/sim/combat-rules.test.ts](https://github.com/ErikBurdett/theandril-hearth-and-card/blob/main/src/sim/combat-rules.test.ts), [tests/browser/combat-rules.spec.ts](https://github.com/ErikBurdett/theandril-hearth-and-card/blob/main/tests/browser/combat-rules.spec.ts), [docs/CARD_RULES.md](https://github.com/ErikBurdett/theandril-hearth-and-card/blob/main/docs/CARD_RULES.md)

- [x] **Completed — Give rivals distinct tactical decisions** (rival-decisions)
  - Current: Every guest pilots their recipe with one of six public personalities: aggressive, defensive, reactive, recursive, evasive or Hero-minded. Personalities change casting priority, removal timing, held response mana, protection tricks, counter thresholds, attack risk, blocking, gang blocks, Hero abilities and attack destinations; all of them swing when the public table shows lethal. Duels without a recognizable guest keep the steady pilot. The duel welcome names the tactics, and the loser of the last meeting opens the next one.
  - Completion boundary: Delivered: six personalities on the twenty-guest roster, scenario tests for removal timing, held counter mana, protection, lethal attacks, useful and gang blocks, evasion and Hero use, and a hidden-information test that swaps the keeper's hand and library without changing any rival decision. Limits: personalities use heuristics, not search, and do not bluff or evaluate every instant-speed trick.
